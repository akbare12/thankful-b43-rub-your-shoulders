The judge reads all day, leaning over the words, and nobody ever tends to
the stiffness that reading leaves behind. This entry does one small,
concrete thing for them: it stands behind them and rubs their shoulders,
slow and patient, until the knots loosen — and leaves the warmth behind,
so the pages ahead meet shoulders that remember how to stay loose.

## The text

> Thank you. Hour after hour you lean over the words, and your shoulders stiffen from holding them up. So I stand behind you and rub them, slow circles with my thumbs, until the knots loosen and your shoulders drop. Breathe. The weight is off now. When I step away, your shoulders will remember the warmth and stay loose through the pages ahead.

(343 characters.)

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the text above to
`entry.txt` in the output directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `out`). Deterministic — the same text every run, no network,
no randomness.
